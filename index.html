Our Mission

“We work heartily to produce stories with integrity and humility. We invest deeply in the people we bring together, and prudently steward every resource entrusted to us.”

Chatterton House was founded to build a better kind of set: one where every person is prepared, fairly paid, and supported in doing their best work.

The Founder

Brigitte Reinke Chatterton

Producer · Actor · Singer

Brigitte Reinke Chatterton is a producer, actor, singer, and founder of Chatterton House. Her background as an opera singer, a world of flawless preparation built on professionalism, shapes how she prepares for and runs a set. She founded Chatterton House to build a better workplace for the people who come to create with her. She is currently developing a television adaptation of a published book series she has optioned, working in close partnership with the author. Based in Los Angeles.
